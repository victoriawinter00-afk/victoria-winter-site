Privacy Policy

This website is intentionally designed to avoid cookies, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, and personalized advertising. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data for marketing purposes.

Information we may collect

When you contact us through the website, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, business information, and the details of your inquiry or consultation request.

How we use information

We use information only to respond to your inquiry, coordinate services, maintain records required for business operations, and communicate about your consultation or project. We do not use or sell your information for targeted advertising or profiling.

Retention

We retain records only for the time period required by applicable law and business necessity. After the applicable retention period expires, we securely destroy records in accordance with Colorado and federal requirements.

No cookies and no tracking

This website does not use cookies, web beacons, advertising pixels, remarketing tags, or third-party tracking scripts. We do not use cross-site tracking tools or personalized ad networks.

Third-party services

We may use secure, minimal service providers necessary to operate the website and process a contact request. These may include email transmission services or website hosting providers, but we do not use invasive advertising or profiling technologies.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time through the consultation form on this website to ask questions about the information we hold, request a copy of your submitted information, or ask that we delete any information we are not legally required to retain. We may need to retain certain information to fulfill legal or professional obligations.

Legal notice

This privacy policy does not replace applicable law or contractual obligations. If you have a specific legal question, consult an attorney or other appropriate licensed professional.

This notice is provided for informational purposes and is intended to be clear, direct, and respectful of your privacy.
