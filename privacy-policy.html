Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider sets strictly necessary security cookies to protect this site. This website uses no third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or personalized advertising. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data for marketing purposes.

Information we may collect

When you contact us through the website, we may receive only the information you choose to provide: your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, selected services, and consultation message. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, medical information, or confidential legal documents through this form.

How we use information

We use information only to respond to your inquiry, coordinate services, prepare or administer a service agreement, maintain records required for business operations, and communicate about your consultation or project. We do not use or sell your information for marketing, targeted advertising, profiling, or marketing email campaigns.

Project and client data

We also hold what is needed to do the work you engage us for: the files and materials you send us (images, logos, fonts, copy), correspondence about the project by email and text, the signed service agreement, the code and content of the site we build for you, and, where payments are taken, payment records held by our payment processor. We use this only to perform the agreement.

Retention

Consultation requests are received at victoria00business00@gmail.com. We keep your records while we work with you. When our work together ends, whether at project completion or when a maintenance plan ends, records related to a service agreement and project files and correspondence are retained for three years, then securely destroyed. The code and content we build are held in a private repository while we maintain your site; on separation our access is destroyed and the repository is yours. Financial records required for tax purposes, including payment records held by our payment processor, are retained for the period required by federal tax law, which is generally seven years. Information is held on secured devices and backed up to a cloud provider, with access limited to the business owner. Records are securely destroyed when the applicable retention period ends.

No tracking or advertising cookies

The only cookies this website uses are strictly necessary security cookies set by our infrastructure provider. We do not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we do not use web beacons, advertising pixels, remarketing tags, third-party tracking scripts, cross-site tracking tools, or personalized ad networks.

Third-party services

This site is hosted and served through Cloudflare Pages. Its contact endpoint runs through Cloudflare Pages Functions. Consultation requests are transmitted through the configured Cloudflare email service binding to victoria00business00@gmail.com. These providers process information only as needed to host the site, operate the contact form, and deliver the message. The demonstration store at store.victoriawinter00.com runs on Cloudflare Workers and a Cloudflare database, and takes no real payment; the only order information recorded is a test order created to demonstrate the confirmation flow. Where real client payments are taken, they are processed by Stripe, which holds the payment data under its own privacy policy; card details never reach our site. We do not use analytics, advertising networks, social media pixels, or behavioral tracking.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time through the consultation form or directly at victoria00business00@gmail.com to ask questions about the information we hold, request a copy of your submitted information, or ask that we delete information we are not legally required to retain; we respond within 30 days and confirm in writing. We may need to retain certain information to fulfill legal, tax, professional, contractual, or business obligations.

Colorado Privacy Act notice

The Colorado Privacy Act applies to entities that process the personal data of 100,000 or more Colorado consumers in a calendar year, or 25,000 or more consumers while deriving revenue from the sale of personal data. Victoria Luna Consulting LLC does not meet these thresholds. We do not sell personal data. We do not process personal data at the volume required for the CPA to apply.

Legal notice

This privacy policy does not replace applicable law or contractual obligations. If you have a specific legal question, consult an attorney or other appropriate licensed professional.

This notice is provided for informational purposes and is intended to be clear, direct, and respectful of your privacy.
