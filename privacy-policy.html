Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This website is intentionally designed to avoid cookies, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, and personalized advertising. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data for marketing purposes.

Information we may collect

When you contact us through the website, we may receive only the information you choose to provide: your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, selected services, and consultation message. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, medical information, or confidential legal documents through this form.

How we use information

We use information only to respond to your inquiry, coordinate services, prepare or administer a service agreement, maintain records required for business operations, and communicate about your consultation or project. We do not use or sell your information for marketing, targeted advertising, profiling, or marketing email campaigns.

Retention

Consultation requests are received at victoria00business00@gmail.com. Records related to a service agreement are retained for three years following project completion, then securely destroyed. Financial records required for tax purposes are retained for the period required by federal tax law, which is generally seven years. Records are securely destroyed when the applicable retention period ends.

No cookies and no tracking

This website does not use cookies, web beacons, advertising pixels, remarketing tags, or third-party tracking scripts. We do not use cross-site tracking tools or personalized ad networks.

Third-party services

This site is hosted and served through Cloudflare Pages. Its contact endpoint runs through Cloudflare Pages Functions. Consultation requests are transmitted through the configured Cloudflare email service binding to victoria00business00@gmail.com. These providers process information only as needed to host the site, operate the contact form, and deliver the message. We do not use analytics, advertising networks, social media pixels, or behavioral tracking.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time through the consultation form or directly at victoria00business00@gmail.com to ask questions about the information we hold, request a copy of your submitted information, or ask that we delete information we are not legally required to retain. We may need to retain certain information to fulfill legal, tax, professional, contractual, or business obligations.

Colorado Privacy Act notice

The Colorado Privacy Act applies to entities that process the personal data of 100,000 or more Colorado consumers in a calendar year, or 25,000 or more consumers while deriving revenue from the sale of personal data. Victoria Luna Consulting LLC does not meet these thresholds. We do not sell personal data. We do not process personal data at the volume required for the CPA to apply.

Legal notice

This privacy policy does not replace applicable law or contractual obligations. If you have a specific legal question, consult an attorney or other appropriate licensed professional.

This notice is provided for informational purposes and is intended to be clear, direct, and respectful of your privacy.
