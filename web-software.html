E-Commerce Webpages

One build per project

These five options are the website builds I offer — three standard builds and two e-commerce builds. Choose the one that fits what you need.

E-commerce builds are scoped by the page and product limits of the platform you choose, Shopify or WooCommerce.

If you need a standard website and an online store, that's two separate websites, scoped and billed as two projects. I'm glad to build both. We'd plan them as separate deliverables and I can list them in a single service agreement or separated agreements.

Service Description: An online store with up to 25 products, configured on Shopify or WooCommerce. Includes platform setup, product pages, product categories, and payment gateway configuration using the platform's built-in tools.

What this means for you: You can start selling online. Customers can browse your products, add them to a cart, and pay by card. The store runs on a platform built for e-commerce, so inventory, customer accounts, and order management are handled by the platform itself.

Platform choice: I'll recommend Shopify or WooCommerce after a short discovery conversation. Shopify is faster to launch and lower-maintenance. WooCommerce gives more control and is better for content-heavy stores. The choice affects which features are available natively versus requiring paid plugins — I'll walk you through the tradeoffs before we commit.

Pricing includes: Platform setup, up to 25 product listings, product pages, product categories, payment gateway configuration (Shopify Payments, WooCommerce Payments, or Stripe/PayPal), platform-native customer accounts, platform-native inventory tracking, mobile responsiveness, and 30 days of post-launch support.

Platform-native features (included if available on your chosen platform): Customer accounts, inventory tracking, subscriptions and recurring billing, multi-language, and multi-currency. Availability depends on platform and, for some features, on which payment gateway you use. I configure what the platform provides natively; I do not write custom code for these features.

Custom integrations: Any integration the platform does not offer natively — custom checkouts, third-party inventory systems, specialized shipping logic, or unique feature requests — is billed separately at $100/hour after a scoped estimate. I reserve the right to decline a custom integration request based on complexity, scope, or my honest capacity to deliver it well.

Multi-language and multi-currency note: Shopify supports multi-language natively (with limits on the free translation app) and multi-currency natively when using Shopify Payments. WooCommerce does not support either natively; both require paid third-party plugins. Neither is configured as part of this scope. If you need them, I'll quote the configuration work separately after confirming what your chosen platform and gateway will require.

Additional time: Work beyond 25 products or outside the stated scope is billed at $100/hour, minimum 1-hour charge, with your approval before work begins.
