Website Maintenance

Included with every maintenance plan. Automated backups, uptime monitoring, error alerting, security and dependency patching, and TLS and DNS. What changes between plans is how many countable units of work are included each cycle and how much investigation is covered.

Units are countable deliverables, never a measure of time. The published equivalence is: 1 content edit = 1 unit · 1 product addition = 1 unit · 1 page added = 3 units. Units outside a plan are $40 for a content edit, $40 for a product addition, and $150 for a page. Unused units carry for one cycle, capped at one cycle's allowance.

Defect remedy: Work required because of an error on our part is corrected at no charge and does not count against the allowance.

How the boundary works: Maintenance keeps the site current and working - it changes what the site says. A project changes what the site is. Requests inside the design you already paid for, describable in one sentence, that add no new capability, are maintenance. Anything else is investigated within the allowance, then quoted in writing, and no work continues before you agree.

Out of scope: New features, new integrations, redesigns, platform migrations, turning payments live, carrier-shipping integration, content beyond the allowance, and third-party fees such as domain renewal and payment-processor charges.

If your business has outgrown standalone tools

ERP systems - SAP, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics - are built for mid-size and large operations, and they're priced accordingly. If you've reached the point where you need one, that's a real milestone, and it deserves a specialist. I don't offer ERP integration. If you get there, I'm glad to point you toward someone who does it well.

How these stores are built and owned

Your store is custom-built on your own infrastructure and billed at cost. There is no platform subscription and no app stack. Stripe's transaction fees, your domain, and any hosting costs are separate. The code belongs to you and is documented for handover, but it is custom code - whoever maintains it will need to be able to read it.

When this is not the right choice

If you need multi-channel selling, point of sale, complex multi-jurisdiction tax and shipping, a very large catalog, or an app ecosystem, a hosted platform is the better answer and we will say so. For those needs, Shopify is the better choice.
